# Aether Pirate Game — design notes

## Vision
- Pirate-era trade, sailing, and faction play
- Player sails around with other NPCs doing the same kinds of activities
- Wants it to look decent, but start basic and expand over time
- Incremental systems: honor, bounties, trading convoys, etc.

## World-first priority
- First milestone is a good-looking world
- Caribbean-inspired map as the starting geography
- Basic boat placeholder okay just so scale, motion, and settlement anchors read correctly
- Art direction goal: stylized but intentional, readable at a glance, atmospheric water/weather feel

## Factions
- Major historical pirate-era nations as independent entities, each fighting and surviving on their own
- Custom nations added by the player/developer on top
- 5 pirate factions
- 1 smuggling faction
- Freelance NPCs in larger numbers with their own motives, tasks, and behavior

## Economy
- Trade goods list, including illegal goods
- Ship list / ship types
- Economy that loops: production, consumption, routes, price pressure between regions

## Open questions still to lock
- 2D/2.5D vs 3D world presentation
- Whether pirate factions start fully separate from historical nations or grow out of them
- First playable slice: sailing/world first, or trading/economy first
